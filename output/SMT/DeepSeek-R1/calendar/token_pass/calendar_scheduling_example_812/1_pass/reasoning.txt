   T = Int('T')
   s = Solver()

   # Constrain T to be in [0, 1890] and also such that the meeting does not go beyond the end of the day.
   # But note: we have to break by day. Actually, we can also express the constraint by:
   #   For each day i (0<=i<=3), if T is in [i*480, i*480+450] then it's valid.

   # But we can also break the constraint by day by using:
   #   (And(T >= i*480, T <= i*480+450) for at least one i.

We are given the task to schedule a meeting for Michael, Eric, and Arthur on Monday between 9:00 and 17:00.
 The meeting duration is 30 minutes.

 Steps:
 1. We'll represent time in minutes from 9:00 (which is 0 minutes) to 17:00 (which is 8*60 = 480 minutes).
 2. We have to avoid the busy times for each person.

 Michael's busy intervals (in minutes from 9:00):
  9:30-10:30 -> (30, 90)
  15:00-15:30 -> (360, 390)
  16:00-16:30 -> (420, 450)

 Eric: free all day.

 Arthur's busy intervals:
  9:00-12:00 -> (0, 180)
  13:00-15:00 -> (240, 360)
  15:30-16:00 -> (390, 420)
  16:30-17:00 -> (450, 480)

 We are to find a 30-minute slot that does not conflict with any of these.

 We'll use Z3 to find a start time S (in minutes from 9:00) such that:
  0 <= S <= 480 - 30 (so that the meeting ends by 17:00)

 And for each person, the meeting [S, S+30] must not overlap with any of their busy intervals.

 We can model non-overlap for a set of busy intervals [b_start, b_end] by:
   For each busy interval: (S + 30 <= b_start) OR (S >= b_end)

 However, note that the busy intervals are given as inclusive of start and exclusive of end?
 But in the problem, the meeting is half an hour, and we are representing time in minutes.

 Since the meeting is half an hour, we can represent the end time as S+30.

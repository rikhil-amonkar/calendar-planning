We are given the work hours from 9:00 to 17:00 (which is 9 AM to 5 PM) on Monday.
 We are to schedule a one-hour meeting for the participants.

 Steps:
 1. Represent time in minutes from 9:00 (so 9:00 is 0, 10:00 is 60, 17:00 is 480).
 2. The meeting duration is 60 minutes.
 3. We have to find a start time (S) such that:
     S >= 0 (9:00) and S + 60 <= 480 (17:00)
 4. For each participant, we have busy intervals. The meeting should not overlap with any busy interval.

 We'll define the busy intervals for each participant in minutes (relative to 9:00).

 We are to find a start time S (in minutes from 9:00) and then convert it back to time.

 We'll use Z3 to solve for S.

 Constraints for S:
   For each participant, for each busy interval [B_start, B_end], we require:
      S + 60 <= B_start OR S >= B_end

 But note: the busy intervals are given as the times when the participant is busy.

 Alternatively, we can say that the meeting must be entirely in the free time.

We are given:
  Participants: Arthur and Michael
  Meeting duration: 30 minutes (0.5 hours)
  Work hours: 9:00 to 17:00 (so 8 hours per day, from 9:00 to 17:00 inclusive of start, exclusive of end for the meeting)
  Days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday

Constraints:
  Arthur cannot meet on Tuesday.

We are to find the earliest available time.

We'll represent time in minutes from 9:00 on each day. So 9:00 is 0, 10:00 is 60, etc.
The work day is 8 hours = 480 minutes (from 0 to 480, but note 17:00 is 480, and we can only schedule until 480 - duration = 450 for a 30-minute meeting).

We have three days: Monday (index 0), Tuesday (index 1), Wednesday (index 2).

We are to assign:
  day: an integer from 0 to 2 (0=Monday, 1=Tuesday, 2=Wednesday)
  start_time: an integer in minutes from 0 to 450 (since 450 + 30 = 480, which is 17:00)

But note: Arthur cannot meet on Tuesday, so we must avoid Tuesday for Arthur.

We'll create a Z3 solver and add constraints for each participant's busy times on each day.

We can represent the overall time as: day * 480 + start, and minimize that.

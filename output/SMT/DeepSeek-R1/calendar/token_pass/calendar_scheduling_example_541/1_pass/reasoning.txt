We are given the task to schedule a one-hour meeting for Kayla and Rebecca on Monday between 9:00 and 17:00.
 We are provided with their existing blocked times.

 Steps:
 1. We'll convert time to minutes from 9:00 (which we consider as minute 0) until 17:00 (which is 8*60 = 480 minutes).
 2. The meeting duration is 60 minutes.
 3. We define the available time for each person by subtracting the blocked times from the work hours.
 4. We'll use Z3 to find a start time (in minutes from 9:00) such that the entire meeting interval (start to start+60) does not overlap with any blocked interval for any participant.

 For Kayla:
   Blocked: 10:00-10:30 -> from (60) to (90) minutes
            14:30-16:00 -> from (330) to (420) minutes

 For Rebecca:
   Blocked: 9:00-13:00 -> from (0) to (240) minutes
            13:30-15:00 -> from (270) to (360) minutes
            15:30-16:00 -> from (390) to (420) minutes

 We are to find a start time S (in minutes from 9:00) such that:
   0 <= S <= 480 - 60 (so that the meeting ends by 17:00)

 And for each participant, the meeting interval [S, S+60] does not overlap with any of their blocked intervals.

 We can model non-overlap for a blocked interval [B_start, B_end] as:
   S >= B_end OR S+60 <= B_start

 We'll combine constraints for all blocked intervals of all participants.

 Since there are multiple participants, we need to satisfy the constraints for every participant.

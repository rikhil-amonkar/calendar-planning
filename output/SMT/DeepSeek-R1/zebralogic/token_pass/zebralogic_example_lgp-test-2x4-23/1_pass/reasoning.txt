 Clues:
 1. The person who owns a Tesla Model 3 is somewhere to the right of Arnold.
    This means: the house of Tesla Model 3 owner has a higher house number than Arnold's house.
 2. Arnold is the person who is short.
    So, the house where Name is 'Arnold' has Height 'short'.
 3. The person whose mother's name is Holly is in the second house.
    So, the house with Mother 'Holly' is house 2.

 Since there are only two houses, we can also note that the mother in house 1 must be the other one (Aniya).

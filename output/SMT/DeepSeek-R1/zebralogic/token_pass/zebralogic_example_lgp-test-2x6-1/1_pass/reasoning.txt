 We have two options for each attribute.

 2. Create Z3 variables for each attribute for each house.
 3. Assert that all attributes are distinct per category (i.e., each name is unique, etc.)

 Clues:
 1. The person who loves soccer is not in the second house.
    => So, soccer lover is in house 1.

 2. The Desert smoothie lover is directly left of the person who is very short.
    => Since there are only two houses, Desert smoothie lover is in house 1 and very short is in house 2.

 3. The person who is very short is the person who has brown hair.
    => So, brown hair is in the same house as very short (which is house 2, from clue 2).

 4. The person who loves a carnations arrangement is the Desert smoothie lover.
    => So, carnations lover is in the same house as Desert smoothie lover (which is house 1, from clue 2).

 5. Eric and the person who has brown hair are next to each other.
    => Since there are two houses, they are adjacent. Brown hair is in house 2 (from clue 3 and 2), so Eric must be in house 1.

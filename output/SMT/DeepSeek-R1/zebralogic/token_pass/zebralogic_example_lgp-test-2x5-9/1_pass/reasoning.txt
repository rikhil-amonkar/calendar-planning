 Since the houses are ordered, we can create variables for each attribute in each house.

   For house i (i=1,2):
     name_i: which can be "Arnold" or "Eric"
     book_i: which can be "science fiction" or "mystery"
     vacation_i: which can be "mountain" or "beach"

 However, Z3 doesn't have built-in strings for all theories, so we will use integers or enums.

 We'll use EnumSort for each category.

 Steps in code:
   Create enums for:
     Name: Arnold, Eric

   Then for each house i, we have a variable for each category.

   Then add the clues.

 Clues:
   1. The person who loves beach vacations is Eric.
      This means: for the house where vacation is beach, the name must be Eric.

   2. The person who loves pop music is the person who loves beach vacations.
      So, the house with pop music is the same as the house with beach vacation.

   3. The person who loves rock music is the person who loves mystery books.
      So, the house with rock music is the same as the house with mystery books.

   4. The cat lover is not in the second house.
      So, animal_2 != cat -> animal_1 must be cat? Actually, since there are two houses, if cat is not in house2, then it must be in house1.

   5. The person who loves mystery books is in the first house.
      So, book_1 = mystery.

 We also have the constraint that each attribute is unique per house.

To solve this scheduling problem, we need to follow these steps:

1. **Parse the Input Data**: Convert the times and constraints into a structured format that can be easily manipulated and compared.
2. **Identify Available Time Slots**: For each participant, identify the time slots during which they are available.
3. **Find Common Availability**: Determine the time slots that all participants are available for the meeting duration.
4. **Apply Constraints**: Filter out any time slots that violate the given constraints (e.g., Bobby prefers not to have meetings after 15:00).
5. **Select a Suitable Time Slot**: Choose one of the valid time slots as the meeting time.
6. **Output the Result**: Print the selected time slot and the day of the week in the specified format.

- **Time Representation**: We'll represent times as integers (e.g., 9:00 AM as 900, 10:30 AM as 1030) to make comparison and arithmetic operations easier.
- **Available Slots Calculation**: For each participant, calculate the gaps between their scheduled meetings as potential available slots.
- **Common Slots Identification**: Use set intersection to find common slots across all participants.
- **Constraint Handling**: After identifying common slots, filter them based on specific constraints (e.g., Bobby's preference to avoid meetings after 15:00).
- **Output Formatting**: Format the selected time slot in the required HH:MM:HH:MM format and print it along with the day of the week.

This approach ensures that we efficiently find a suitable meeting time that respects all participants' schedules and preferences.

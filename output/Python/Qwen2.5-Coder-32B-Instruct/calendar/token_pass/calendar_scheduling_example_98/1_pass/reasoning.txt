2. **Exclude Busy Times**: For each participant, exclude the times they are busy from their available time slots.

3. **Find Common Free Slots**: After determining the free time slots for each participant, find the overlapping time slots where all participants are free.

4. **Check Meeting Duration**: Ensure that the common free slot is at least as long as the required meeting duration (30 minutes in this case).

5. **Output the Solution**: Once a suitable time slot is found, output the time range and the day of the week in the specified format.

- **Step 1**: We know the work hours are from 9:00 to 17:00. We will consider this as our base time slots.
- **Step 2**: We will subtract the busy times from each participant's work hours.
- **Step 3**: To find the common free slots, we can use a set intersection approach.
- **Step 4**: We will check if any of the intersected time slots are at least 30 minutes long.
- **Step 5**: Finally, we will print out the first valid time slot found along with the day of the week.

To solve this Zebra Puzzle, we need to logically deduce the arrangement of people, their birthdays, and their mothers' names based on the given clues. Let's break down the problem:

1. **Identify Variables and Constraints**:
   - Houses: 1, 2
   - Names: Arnold, Eric
   - Birthdays: april, sept
   - Mothers: Aniya, Holly

2. **Clues Analysis**:
   - Clue 1: Eric is somewhere to the left of the person whose mother's name is Holly.
     - This means if Holly is in house 1, Eric must be in house 2. If Holly is in house 2, Eric can be in either house 1 or 2, but since he must be to the left, he must be in house 1.
   - Clue 2: The person whose birthday is in April is in the first house.
     - This directly places the person with the April birthday in house 1.

3. **Logical Deduction**:
   - From Clue 2, we know the person in house 1 has a birthday in April.
   - From Clue 1, if Eric is in house 1, his mother cannot be Holly (since Holly would have to be to his right). Therefore, Eric must be in house 1, and his mother must be Aniya.
   - This leaves Arnold for house 2, with a birthday in September (since April is already taken), and his mother must be Holly (as she must be to the right of Eric).

4. **Construct the Solution**:
   - House 1: Eric, April, Aniya
   - House 2: Arnold, Sept, Holly

3. **Apply the Constraint:**
   - We need to check which of the possible assignments satisfy the constraint: Eric is directly left of the person who loves mystery books.
   - This means if Eric is in house 1, the person in house 2 must love mystery books.

4. **Generate All Possible Combinations:**
   - Generate all possible combinations of people and book genres for the two houses.
   - Filter out the combinations that do not satisfy the given constraint.

5. **Format the Output:**

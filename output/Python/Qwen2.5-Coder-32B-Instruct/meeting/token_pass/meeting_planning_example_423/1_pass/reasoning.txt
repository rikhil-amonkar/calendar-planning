   - Our goal is to create an itinerary that respects these constraints and maximizes the number of meetings.

2. **Model the Problem:**

   - Represent the meeting constraints as intervals (start time, end time) and required durations.

3. **Algorithm Design:**
   - Use a greedy approach to find a feasible schedule.
   - Sort potential meeting times based on their start times.
   - Try to fit meetings into the schedule, respecting travel times and minimum meeting durations.
   - If a meeting cannot be scheduled due to conflicts or insufficient time, skip it.
   - Continue until no more meetings can be added without violating constraints.

4. **Implementation Details:**

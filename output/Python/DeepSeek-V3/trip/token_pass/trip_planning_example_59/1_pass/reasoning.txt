Let’s find integer solution:

Let a = days in Bucharest alone (not overlapping with Lyon),  
b = days in Lyon alone,  
c = days in Porto alone,  
d = days in Bucharest+Lyon (day 7),  
e = days in Lyon+Porto (flight days).

We have:  
Bucharest total = a + d = 7  
Lyon total = b + d + e = 7  
Porto total = c + e = 4  
Total distinct days = a + b + c + d + e = 16.

From Bucharest total: a = 7 - d.  
From Porto total: c = 4 - e.  
Plug into distinct days:  
(7-d) + b + (4-e) + d + e = 16  
7 + 4 + b = 16 → b = 5.

Then Lyon total: b + d + e = 7 → 5 + d + e = 7 → d + e = 2.

Also d ≥ 1 (day 7 overlap B-L), e ≥ 0.  
So possible: d=1, e=1 or d=2, e=0.

Case 1: d=1, e=1:  
Then a=6, b=5, c=3, d=1, e=1.  
So: Bucharest alone 6 days, Lyon alone 5 days, Porto alone 3 days, B+L day 1, L+P day 1.

That means:  
Bucharest days = 6 alone + 1 overlap = 7.  
Lyon days = 5 alone + 2 overlap = 7.  
Porto days = 3 alone + 1 overlap = 4.  
Total distinct = 6+5+3+1+1=16.

Now we must schedule it:  
Start Bucharest day 1.  
Bucharest alone days 1–6.  
Day 7: B+L overlap (fly to Lyon).  
Lyon alone days 8–12 (5 days).  
Day 13: L+P overlap (fly to Porto).  
Porto alone days 14–16 (3 days).  
Check: Porto total = day 13 (overlap) + 14,15,16 = 4 days ✅.  
Lyon total = day 7 + 8–12 + day 13 = 1+5+1=7 ✅.  
Bucharest total = 1–6 + day 7 = 7 ✅.  
All good.

So itinerary:

Let’s break down the problem first.

**Given constraints:**
- Total days: 7
- Must visit: Riga (2 days), Amsterdam (2 days), Mykonos (5 days)  
  → Sum of desired days = 2 + 2 + 5 = 9 days, but total trip is only 7 days.  
  This means there is overlap because travel days count for both cities.
- Direct flights exist only between: Amsterdam–Mykonos, Riga–Amsterdam.
- You must be in Riga between day 1 and day 2 (so day 1 or day 2 must be in Riga, likely meaning you are there at the start).
- You spend 2 days in Riga, 2 days in Amsterdam, 5 days in Mykonos, but total is 7 → overlap means some days count for two cities.

**Overlap rule:** If you fly from A to B on day X, you are in both A and B on day X.  
So a travel day counts toward the total for both cities.

**Graph of connections:**
- Riga ↔ Amsterdam ↔ Mykonos  
No direct Riga–Mykonos, so to go from Riga to Mykonos, you must go via Amsterdam.

**Goal:** Arrange 7 days so that each city gets its required days counting overlaps.

---

Let’s think:  
Let’s denote days 1..7.  
Let’s try to satisfy Riga first:  
We need Riga on 2 days. Since Riga is only connected to Amsterdam, any entry/exit from Riga is via Amsterdam.

Possible sequence:  
Start in Riga day 1 → Amsterdam day 1 (travel) → Amsterdam day 2 → Mykonos day 2 (travel) → Mykonos days 3–7? Let’s check counts:

Day 1: Riga + Amsterdam (travel from Riga to Amsterdam) → Riga 1 day, Amsterdam 1 day.  
Day 2: Amsterdam + Mykonos (travel from Amsterdam to Mykonos) → Amsterdam 2 days total, Mykonos 1 day.  
Days 3–7: Mykonos only → Mykonos gets 5 more days → total Mykonos = 6 days, but we need exactly 5. So too many.

We need exactly 5 days in Mykonos, 2 in Amsterdam, 2 in Riga.

Let’s define variables:  
Let \( R \) = days exclusively in Riga (no travel)  
Let \( A \) = days exclusively in Amsterdam (no travel)  
Let \( M \) = days exclusively in Mykonos (no travel)  
Let \( RA \) = days in both Riga and Amsterdam (travel between them)  
Let \( AM \) = days in both Amsterdam and Mykonos (travel between them)  
Let \( RM \) = 0 (no direct flight).

We have:  
\[
R + RA = 2 \quad\text{(Riga total days)}
\]
\[
A + RA + AM = 2 \quad\text{(Amsterdam total days)}
\]
\[
M + AM = 5 \quad\text{(Mykonos total days)}
\]
\[
R + A + M + RA + AM = 7 \quad\text{(total distinct days in schedule)}
\]

From \( R + RA = 2 \) → \( RA = 2 - R \)  
From \( M + AM = 5 \) → \( AM = 5 - M \)

Plug into Amsterdam equation:  
\( A + (2 - R) + (5 - M) = 2 \) → \( A + 7 - R - M = 2 \) → \( A - R - M = -5 \) → \( R + M - A = 5 \).  

Total days equation:  
\( R + A + M + (2 - R) + (5 - M) = 7 \) → \( R + A + M + 7 - R - M = 7 \) → \( A + 7 = 7 \) → \( A = 0 \).

So \( A = 0 \) means no exclusive Amsterdam day, all Amsterdam days are travel days.

Then \( R + M - 0 = 5 \) → \( R + M = 5 \).

Also \( R \ge 0, M \ge 0 \), and \( RA = 2 - R \ge 0 \) → \( R \le 2 \),  
\( AM = 5 - M \ge 0 \) → \( M \le 5 \).

We also need sequence feasible with direct flights:  
Possible patterns:  
1. Start in Riga (R or RA day) → Amsterdam (RA or A day) → Mykonos (AM or M day) → possibly back to Amsterdam → possibly back to Riga.

But A=0 means Amsterdam only appears as RA or AM.

Let’s pick R=2, then RA=0, M=3, AM=2.

Check:  
R=2 (exclusive Riga days), RA=0, A=0, AM=2, M=3.  
Riga total = 2, Amsterdam total = 0+0+2=2, Mykonos total = 3+2=5. Total distinct days = 2+0+3+0+2=7.

Sequence:  
Day 1: R (Riga only)  
Day 2: R (Riga only)  
Day 3: RA? Wait RA=0 in our numbers, so we can’t have RA. That means no travel between Riga and Amsterdam at all? Then how to reach Mykonos? We must go Riga→Amsterdam→Mykonos, so at least one RA day. So RA≥1. So R≤1.

Let’s pick R=1, RA=1, M=4, AM=1.  
Check:  
Riga total = 1+1=2, Amsterdam total = 0+1+1=2, Mykonos total = 4+1=5. Total days = 1+0+4+1+1=7.

Sequence:  
Day 1: R (Riga only)  
Day 2: RA (Riga→Amsterdam travel)  
Day 3: AM (Amsterdam→Mykonos travel)  
Days 4–7: M (Mykonos only)  

This works:  
Day 1: Riga only  
Day 2: Riga + Amsterdam  
Day 3: Amsterdam + Mykonos  
Days 4–7: Mykonos only  

Counts: Riga: day1, day2 → 2 days. Amsterdam: day2, day3 → 2 days. Mykonos: day3,4,5,6,7 → 5 days.

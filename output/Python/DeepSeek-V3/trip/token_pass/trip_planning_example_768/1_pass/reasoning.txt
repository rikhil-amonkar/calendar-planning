Let's break this down step-by-step before writing the code.

**Step 1 – Understanding the constraints**

We have 6 cities:  
- Mykonos (M) – 4 days  
- Nice (N) – 3 days total, but conference on days 14 and 16 (so Nice must include those days)  
- London (L) – 2 days  
- Copenhagen (C) – 3 days  
- Oslo (O) – 5 days, with friend visit between day 10 and 14 (so at least some Oslo days in that range)  
- Tallinn (T) – 4 days  

Total days = 4 + 3 + 2 + 3 + 5 + 4 = 21 days, but we only have 16 days.  
That means some days overlap due to travel days counting for both cities.

**Step 2 – Travel day counting rule**  
If you fly from A to B on day X, you are in A in the morning and B in the evening (or vice versa), so day X counts for both cities toward their required days.  
Thus, total days in itinerary = required days in cities − number of flights.

Let’s check:  
Required city days sum = 21.  
We have 16 calendar days.  
So we need 21 − 16 = 5 travel days (flights) where a day is double-counted.

**Step 3 – Direct flights graph**  
From the list:  
- L ↔ C  
- C ↔ T  
- T ↔ O  
- M ↔ L  
- O ↔ N  
- L ↔ N  
- M ↔ N  
- L ↔ O  
- C ↔ N  
- C ↔ O  

So it’s a fairly connected graph.

**Step 4 – Fixed Nice constraints**  
Nice: conference on day 14 and day 16.  
Since day 16 is the last day, Nice must be the last city (or we leave Nice on day 16 evening, but that still counts as Nice on day 16).  
Also, Nice total = 3 days. So if days 14 and 16 are Nice, then the 3rd Nice day could be day 15 or another day earlier.

**Step 5 – Oslo constraint**  
Oslo friend between day 10 and 14. So at least one Oslo day in [10,14].

**Step 6 – Approach**  
We can treat this as a scheduling/sequencing problem:  
We need to arrange visits to cities in some order, with stays of certain lengths, respecting flight connections, and matching total days = 16.

Because the graph is connected, the main challenge is fitting the Nice conference days.

Let’s try constructing logically:

We have 16 days: Day 1 to Day 16.  
Day 14 and 16 in Nice.  
If Nice total = 3 days, then maybe Nice days: 14, 15, 16. That means we arrive in Nice by day 14 morning (or earlier) and stay through day 16.

Oslo constraint: at least one Oslo day in 10–14.  
If Nice is 14–16, Oslo could be before Nice, e.g., Oslo on day 13, then fly Oslo→Nice (direct flight exists) for day 14.

**Overlap rule:**  
If you fly from A to B on day X, you are in both A and B on day X.  
So travel days count toward both cities’ totals.

- Manchester: 3 days (1, 2, 3)
- Venice: 7 days (3–9)
- Istanbul: 7 days total

- All consecutive stays in a city must be connected by direct flights when switching.

**Step 1 — Understanding the constraints**

We have 8 cities to visit in 18 days:

1. **Mykonos** — 4 days, between day 15 and day 18 (so days 15–18 inclusive).
2. **Krakow** — 5 days.
3. **Vilnius** — 2 days.
4. **Helsinki** — 2 days.
5. **Dubrovnik** — 3 days, with an annual show from day 2 to day 4 (so Dubrovnik must be visited on days 2, 3, and 4).
6. **Oslo** — 2 days, with friends between day 1 and day 2 (so Oslo must include day 1 and/or day 2).
7. **Madrid** — 5 days.
8. **Paris** — 2 days.

Total days = 4 + 5 + 2 + 2 + 3 + 2 + 5 + 2 = 25 days, but we only have 18 days.  
That means some days must overlap due to travel days counting for both cities.

**Step 2 — Travel day rule**

If you fly from A to B on day X, you are in A in the morning and B in the evening, so day X counts for both cities toward their required days.  
This means we can satisfy two cities’ required days with one calendar day if we travel that day.

**Step 3 — Fixed date constraints**

- Day 2–4: Dubrovnik (3 days fixed).
- Day 15–18: Mykonos (4 days fixed).
- Day 1–2: Oslo (must include at least one of these days for Oslo’s 2 days).

**Step 6 — Algorithm outline**

We can treat this as a constraint satisfaction problem:  
We have 18 days, each day we are in one city (if no travel) or two cities (if travel).  
We must satisfy min days per city.

We have Oslo covering day 1 or 2.

We can brute-force search over permutations of cities between fixed blocks, ensuring direct flights between consecutive cities in the sequence.

**Step 7 — Implementation plan**

We’ll write a search over sequences of stays (city, start_day, end_day) with travel days overlapping.

Given complexity, we’ll do a backtracking search:

- Start with day 1, pick a city for first stay (must be Oslo because Oslo must include day 1 or 2, easiest to start Oslo day 1).
- Ensure direct flights between consecutive cities.
- When we place a stay, it consumes days for that city.
- Travel days count for both cities.
- Fixed stays: Dubrovnik days 2–4, Mykonos days 15–18.
- We’ll try to fill days 1–18 with stays covering all requirements.

Let’s break down the problem and then write the code.

**Step-by-step reasoning:**

1. **Total days:** 10 days (Day 1 to Day 10).
2. **City constraints:**
   - London: exactly 3 days total (can be non-consecutive, but must account for travel days).
   - Santorini: exactly 6 days total, with conferences on Day 5 and Day 10.
   - Istanbul: exactly 3 days total.
3. **Direct flights:**
   - Istanbul ↔ London
   - London ↔ Santorini
   - No direct Istanbul ↔ Santorini.
4. **Travel rule:** If you fly from A to B on day X, you are in both A and B on day X (counts toward totals for both cities).
5. **Implication:** To move between Istanbul and Santorini, you must go via London, which will take an extra travel day.

---

**Key constraints:**
- Day 5: Must be in Santorini (conference).
- Day 10: Must be in Santorini (conference).
- Santorini total = 6 days.
- London total = 3 days.
- Istanbul total = 3 days.

---

**Let’s try to schedule:**

We have 10 days, each day counts toward exactly one or two cities (if traveling).  
If we travel on a day, that day counts for both origin and destination.

We need to arrange the sequence of cities to meet totals.

---

**Possible approach:**
Start in London (Day 1), then go to Santorini, then Istanbul, or some other order, but must end in Santorini on Day 10.

Let’s test a feasible sequence:

We know Day 5 = Santorini, Day 10 = Santorini.

Let’s try:
- Days 1–3: London (3 days London done).
- Day 4: Travel London → Santorini (counts as London and Santorini).
- Days 5–9: Santorini (Day 5 conference, plus Days 6,7,8,9 in Santorini).
- But then Istanbul not visited yet, and no direct Santorini–Istanbul flight.  
  So must go Santorini → London → Istanbul, which adds travel days.

Better to visit Istanbul before final Santorini stay.

---

**Let’s attempt a valid numeric allocation:**

Let’s denote:
- L = London days
- S = Santorini days
- I = Istanbul days
- T = travel days (where day counts for 2 cities)

We have:
L + S + I - T = 10  (because each travel day is counted twice in L+S+I, so subtract T to get actual days)
And:
L = 3
S = 6
I = 3

So 3 + 6 + 3 - T = 10 → 12 - T = 10 → T = 2 travel days.

So exactly 2 days involve flying.

---

**Sequence planning with 2 travel days:**

We start somewhere on Day 1, end in Santorini on Day 10.

Possible route:  
Istanbul → London → Santorini (2 travel days: Istanbul–London, London–Santorini).

Check totals:
- Start Day 1 Istanbul.
- Travel Day X: Istanbul → London (counts for Istanbul and London).
- Stay in London until travel Day Y: London → Santorini (counts for London and Santorini).
- Stay in Santorini until Day 10.

We need Istanbul total = 3, London total = 3, Santorini total = 6.

Let’s assign:
Day 1–3: Istanbul (3 days Istanbul done).
Day 4: Travel Istanbul → London (counts for Istanbul and London) → now Istanbul = 4 days? Wait, that would exceed 3. So we can’t have 3 full days in Istanbul plus a travel day from Istanbul, because travel day adds to Istanbul count.

So we must adjust: If we want Istanbul total = 3, and we have a travel day from Istanbul, then we can only have 2 full days in Istanbul before travel day.

Similarly for London: if we have a travel day to London and a travel day from London, those two days count for London, so we only need 1 full day in London to reach total 3.

---

**Let’s try concrete schedule:**

Day 1–2: Istanbul (2 days Istanbul).
Day 3: Travel Istanbul → London (counts Istanbul and London) → Istanbul = 3 done, London = 1.
Day 4: London (full day) → London = 2.
Day 5: Travel London → Santorini (counts London and Santorini) → London = 3 done, Santorini = 1.
Day 6–10: Santorini (Days 6,7,8,9,10 = 5 days) + Day 5 travel day = Santorini total = 6.

Check:
Istanbul: Day 1,2,3 = 3 days.
London: Day 3,4,5 = 3 days.
Santorini: Day 5,6,7,8,9,10 = 6 days.
All constraints met: Day 5 and 10 in Santorini, totals correct, direct flights used.

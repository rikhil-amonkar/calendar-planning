Let’s break this down step by step before writing the code.

**Given:**
- Total days: 12
- Riga: 5 days
- Vilnius: 7 days
- Dublin: 2 days
- Direct flights: Dublin ↔ Riga, Riga ↔ Vilnius  
- No direct flight Dublin ↔ Vilnius
- Travel days count toward both cities’ totals.

**Logical constraints:**
1. Dublin is only connected to Riga, so to visit all three cities, the sequence must be Dublin → Riga → Vilnius or reverse.
2. Since Riga is the hub, the trip must start or end in Dublin, go to Riga, then to Vilnius (or reverse).
3. We must allocate days so that each city gets its required days, including travel days.

**Approach:**
- Let’s treat travel days as days spent partly in both cities.
- If you fly from A to B on day X, day X counts for both A and B totals.
- So total days in itinerary = 12, but sum of days per city > 12 because of double-counting travel days.

Let’s define:
- `d` = days solely in Dublin
- `r` = days solely in Riga
- `v` = days solely in Vilnius
- Travel days: 1 from Dublin to Riga, 1 from Riga to Vilnius.

Let’s denote:
Day in Dublin before flight to Riga = travel day 1 (counts for Dublin and Riga).
Day in Riga before flight to Vilnius = travel day 2 (counts for Riga and Vilnius).

So:
Dublin total = d + 1  
Riga total = r + 2  
Vilnius total = v + 1  

We know:
Dublin total = 2 → d + 1 = 2 → d = 1  
Riga total = 5 → r + 2 = 5 → r = 3  
Vilnius total = 7 → v + 1 = 7 → v = 6  

Check total unique days:  
d + r + v + 2 travel days = 1 + 3 + 6 + 2 = 12 ✅

**Itinerary structure:**
Start in Dublin: 1 full day + travel day to Riga (day counts for Dublin and Riga)  
Then in Riga: 3 full days + travel day to Vilnius (day counts for Riga and Vilnius)  
Then in Vilnius: 6 full days.

**Day mapping:**
Day 1: Dublin only  
Day 2: Dublin (morning) → Riga (evening) → counts for both Dublin and Riga  
Day 3–5: Riga only (3 days)  
Day 6: Riga (morning) → Vilnius (evening) → counts for both Riga and Vilnius  
Day 7–12: Vilnius only (6 days)

But careful: Day ranges in output should be contiguous per location.

Better to group by location stay:
- Dublin: Day 1–2 (Day 2 is travel to Riga, but still counts for Dublin)
- Riga: Day 2–6 (Day 2 arrival, Day 6 departure)
- Vilnius: Day 6–12 (Day 6 arrival, Day 12 end)

Check days count:
Dublin: Day 1, Day 2 morning = 2 days ✅  
Riga: Day 2 evening, Day 3,4,5, Day 6 morning = 5 days ✅  
Vilnius: Day 6 evening, Day 7–12 = 7 days ✅

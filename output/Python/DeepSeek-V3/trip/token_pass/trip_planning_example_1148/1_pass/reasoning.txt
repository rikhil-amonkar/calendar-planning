We need to arrange visits to 8 cities with given constraints, ensuring travel is only via direct flights, and respecting day ranges for events.  

Let’s restate the problem in a structured way:

**Cities and required days:**
1. Lisbon — 2 days (workshop between day 4–5)
2. Dubrovnik — 5 days
3. Copenhagen — 5 days
4. Prague — 3 days
5. Tallinn — 2 days (friend between day 1–2)
6. Stockholm — 4 days (wedding between day 13–16)
7. Split — 3 days
8. Lyon — 2 days (annual show day 18–19)

**Direct flights** (undirected edges):
- Dubrovnik ↔ Stockholm
- Lisbon ↔ Copenhagen
- Lisbon ↔ Lyon
- Copenhagen ↔ Stockholm
- Copenhagen ↔ Split
- Prague ↔ Stockholm
- Tallinn ↔ Stockholm
- Prague ↔ Lyon
- Lisbon ↔ Stockholm
- Prague ↔ Lisbon
- Stockholm ↔ Split
- Prague ↔ Copenhagen
- Split ↔ Lyon
- Copenhagen ↔ Dubrovnik
- Prague ↔ Split
- Tallinn ↔ Copenhagen
- Tallinn ↔ Prague

**Total days:** 19 days exactly.

---

## Step 1 — Understanding constraints

We have fixed events:
- Tallinn: day 1–2 (so starts in Tallinn)
- Lisbon: day 4–5 (so must be in Lisbon on day 4 and 5)
- Stockholm: day 13–16 (must be in Stockholm on those days)
- Lyon: day 18–19 (must be in Lyon on those days)

We also have required total days per city, so we can’t split visits arbitrarily without checking connectivity.

---

## Step 2 — Approach

We can treat this as a **constraint satisfaction problem**:
- Days are numbered 1 to 19.
- We assign a city to each day.
- Travel between cities allowed only if direct flight exists.
- Total days per city must match requirement.
- Event days fixed.

We can solve via **backtracking search** with pruning.

---

## Step 3 — Implementation plan

We’ll:
1. Represent the graph of direct flights.
2. Start with fixed events in schedule.
3. Fill remaining days via backtracking, ensuring:
   - Adjacent days either same city or connected by flight.
   - Total days per city matches requirement.
   - No city visited in non-consecutive blocks unless necessary (but here, to simplify, we allow multiple visits if needed, but that might increase travel days; we’ll try to keep visits consecutive to save travel days).
4. Output as JSON with day ranges.

We need to plan a 16-day trip to 8 cities with specific constraints on when certain events happen and how many days to spend in each city.  
Let’s break this down step by step before writing the code.

---

## **Step 1 – Understanding the constraints**

Cities and required days:

1. **Vienna** – 4 days  
2. **Barcelona** – 2 days  
3. **Edinburgh** – 4 days, with a friend visit between day 12 and day 15  
4. **Krakow** – 3 days  
5. **Riga** – 4 days  
6. **Hamburg** – 2 days, with a conference on day 10 and day 11  
7. **Paris** – 2 days, wedding on day 1–2  
8. **Stockholm** – 2 days, relatives on day 15–16  

Total days: 4+2+4+3+4+2+2+2 = 23 days, but we only have 16 days.  
That means some days will be counted for two cities (travel days count for both origin and destination).

---

## **Step 2 – Fixed events in time**

- Day 1–2: Paris (wedding)  
- Day 10–11: Hamburg (conference)  
- Day 12–15: Edinburgh (friend visit window, but not necessarily all days)  
- Day 15–16: Stockholm (relatives)

So we can lock in:

- Day 1: Paris  
- Day 2: Paris  
- Day 10: Hamburg  
- Day 11: Hamburg  
- Day 15: Stockholm  
- Day 16: Stockholm  

---

## **Step 3 – Travel rule**

If you fly from A to B on day X, you spend day X in both cities.  
So travel days help meet multiple city day requirements.

We need to assign each of the 16 days to one or two cities (travel day), such that each city gets its required days, and direct flights exist between consecutive cities in the itinerary.

## **Step 5 – Approach for the program**

We can treat this as a **constraint satisfaction problem**:

- We have 16 days, each day assigned to 1 or 2 cities (if travel).
- Travel only allowed if direct flight exists.
- Fixed events as above.
- Each city’s total days >= required days.

We can solve with **backtracking search**:
- Represent schedule as list of cities per day (length 1 or 2).
- Start with fixed days.
- Fill rest trying to meet day counts.

But since it’s small (16 days, 8 cities), we can brute-force with pruning.

   - Travel connections valid.
   - Day counts met by end.
4. Output first valid schedule found.

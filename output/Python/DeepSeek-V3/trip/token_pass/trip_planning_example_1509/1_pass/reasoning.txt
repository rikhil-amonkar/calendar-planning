We need to build a day-by-day schedule.

Let’s think step-by-step:

**Fixed events:**
- Days 12–13: Santorini
- Days 17–18: Krakow
- Days 23–24: Riga
- Days 4–8: Paris
- Days 18–22: Helsinki

Check conflicts:  
Day 18 is both Krakow (workshop) and Helsinki (friend) — impossible unless they are same city or travel day counts for both. But requirement: Krakow workshop days 17–18 means full days in Krakow on 17 and 18. Helsinki friend days 18–22 means in Helsinki days 18–22. Day 18 cannot be in both unless we fly on day 18 from Krakow to Helsinki, then day 18 counts for both. That’s allowed by rule.

So: Day 18: morning in Krakow, fly to Helsinki, evening in Helsinki — satisfies both: Krakow gets day 18, Helsinki gets day 18.

Similarly, other overlaps:  
Day 23–24 Riga wedding, no other fixed events.

Day 4–8 Paris, no other fixed events.

Day 12–13 Santorini, no other fixed events.

---

**Total days sum:**  
5 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 2 + 4 = 34 days required, but we only have 25 days.  
This means overlap from travel days is essential — we must use travel days to double-count for two cities’ required days.

We need to fit 34 “city-days” into 25 calendar days, so we need at least 34 - 25 = 9 travel days that double-count.

Each travel day can cover 2 cities, so if we have T travel days, total city-days covered = 25 + T.  
We need 25 + T ≥ 34 → T ≥ 9. So exactly 9 travel days, meaning 16 days are single-city days.

**Given constraints:**
- Total days: 21
- City stays:
  - Reykjavik: 7 days
  - Riga: 2 days
  - Warsaw: 3 days
  - Istanbul: 6 days
  - Krakow: 7 days
- Direct flights (undirected graph):
  - Istanbul ↔ Krakow
  - Warsaw ↔ Reykjavik
  - Istanbul ↔ Warsaw
  - Riga ↔ Istanbul
  - Krakow ↔ Warsaw
  - Riga ↔ Warsaw
- Special constraints:
  - Meet friend in Riga between day 1 and day 2 → so Riga must include day 1 or day 2 (or both).
  - Attend wedding in Istanbul between day 2 and day 7 → Istanbul must include some days in [day 2, day 3, …, day 7].
- Travel rule: If you fly from A to B on day X, you are in both cities on day X (so day X counts toward both cities’ totals).

**Step 1 – Understanding the travel day counting**
If we have 5 cities and 4 flights between them, each travel day is counted twice (once for each city).  
So total “city-days” = sum of stays + travel days.  
Let’s denote stays: Rvk=7, Riga=2, Warsaw=3, Istanbul=6, Krakow=7.  
Sum = 25 days.  
But we only have 21 calendar days.  
Difference = 4 days → those are travel days counted twice.  
So number of flights = 4 (because each flight adds 1 extra day to the total city-days count).

We must take exactly 4 flights.

**Step 2 – Graph and possible route**
We need to start somewhere, end somewhere, visit all cities, with exactly 4 flights (so 5 city visits in sequence, possibly revisiting? No, each city once except maybe transit? But we must spend exact days in each, so no city revisited because that would split its stay).

So path: City1 → City2 → City3 → City4 → City5, with 4 flights, visiting all 5 cities exactly once.

**Step 9 – Write Python program**
We’ll:

2. Check if consecutive cities have direct flights.
3. For each valid path, try to assign days satisfying:

But “travel day counted in both cities” means if we fly on day X from A to B, day X counts for both A and B totals. So when allocating, we must ensure the overlap.

Simpler: We just treat travel as happening at the end of the day, so day X is fully in city A, day X+1 starts in city B. But problem says: “if one flies from city A to city B on day X, then they are in both cities A and B on day X”. That means travel day is double-counted.

So in schedule: Start day 1 in first city, stay D1 days (including last day as travel day? No, travel day is separate). Actually, if we stay in city A for S days, then travel to B on day S+1, then day S+1 counts for both A and B. So each travel day adds 1 to total city-days.

Thus: Itinerary: City1: days 1..a (a days), travel day a+1 to City2, City2: days a+1..b (b-a days), etc.

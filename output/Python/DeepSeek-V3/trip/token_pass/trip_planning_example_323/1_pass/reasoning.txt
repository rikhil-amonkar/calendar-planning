Let’s break down the problem before writing the code.

**Given constraints:**
- Total days: 16
- Cities to visit: Split (5 days), Oslo (2 days), London (7 days), Porto (5 days)
- Direct flights exist between:
  - London ↔ Oslo
  - Split ↔ Oslo
  - Oslo ↔ Porto
  - London ↔ Split
- Must attend a show in Split from day 7 to day 11 (inclusive).
- Must visit relatives in London between day 1 and day 7 (inclusive).
- Travel days count toward days in both cities (if you fly on day X, you spend part of day X in origin and part in destination).

**Step 1 – Understanding overlapping constraints**
- Split show: days 7–11 → Split must include those 5 days.
- London relatives: days 1–7 → London must include some days in that range.
- Total days per city:  
  Split = 5 days total (already fixed as days 7–11 if we take it literally, but could be longer if we include travel days overlapping with those).  
  London = 7 days total.  
  Oslo = 2 days total.  
  Porto = 5 days total.  
  Sum = 5 + 7 + 2 + 5 = 19 days, but we only have 16 days total → means some days are double-counted due to travel.

**Step 2 – Travel double-counting**
If you fly on day X, that day counts for both cities. So total "city-days" = 19, but calendar days = 16, so we need 3 travel days (each travel day reduces unique calendar days by 1 because it’s counted twice).

We need exactly 3 travel days to make 19 city-days fit into 16 calendar days.

**Step 3 – Sequence planning**
We must start in London (day 1–7 constraint).  
We must be in Split on day 7–11.  
So:  
- Start in London for some days including day 1.  
- Travel to Split by day 7 at latest.  
- After Split, go to Oslo or Porto.  
- End somewhere.

**Step 4 – Direct flights constraint**
Possible direct connections:  
London ↔ Split  
London ↔ Oslo  
Split ↔ Oslo  
Oslo ↔ Porto  

No direct London–Porto or Split–Porto, so to reach Porto, must go via Oslo.

**Step 5 – Build schedule**
Let’s try:  
1. Start London (day 1–6) → 6 days in London.  
2. Day 7: travel London → Split (counts as day 7 in both London and Split).  
   Now London total = 7 days (days 1–7).  
   Split starts day 7.  
3. Split days 7–11 → that’s days 7,8,9,10,11 in Split = 5 Split days total.  
4. Day 12: travel Split → Oslo (counts as day 12 in both Split and Oslo).  
   Now Split total = 6 days (too many! We only want 5).  
   So we can’t travel on day 12 from Split, because that adds an extra Split day.  
   Instead, we must leave Split on day 11 (but day 11 is last show day, okay).  
   So:  
   Day 11: travel Split → Oslo (counts as day 11 in both Split and Oslo).  
   Now Split days = days 7,8,9,10,11 = 5 total. Good.  
   Oslo starts day 11.  
5. Oslo: need 2 days total. Days 11 and 12 in Oslo = 2 days.  
6. Day 13: travel Oslo → Porto (counts as day 13 in both Oslo and Porto).  
   Now Oslo total = 3 days (too many! We only want 2).  
   So we must leave Oslo earlier:  
   Actually, Oslo needs exactly 2 days. If we arrive Oslo day 11, leave day 12, that’s 2 days (day 11 and 12 in Oslo).  
   Day 12: travel Oslo → Porto (counts as day 12 in both Oslo and Porto).  
   Now Oslo total = 2 days (good).  
7. Porto: need 5 days total. Arrive day 12, stay days 12,13,14,15,16 = 5 days.  
   End day 16 in Porto.

**Step 6 – Check totals**
- London: days 1–7 (day 7 travel) = 7 days.  
- Split: days 7–11 (day 11 travel) = 5 days.  
- Oslo: days 11–12 (day 12 travel) = 2 days.  
- Porto: days 12–16 = 5 days.  

Travel days: day 7 (L→S), day 11 (S→O), day 12 (O→P) = 3 travel days.  
Total city-days = 7 + 5 + 2 + 5 = 19.  
Calendar days = 16.  
Difference = 3 travel days, correct.

**Step 7 – Verify constraints**
- London between day 1–7: yes.  
- Split show day 7–11: yes (in Split all those days).  
- Direct flights:  
  London–Split ✓  
  Split–Oslo ✓  
  Oslo–Porto ✓  

All good.

Let's break this down step-by-step before writing the code.

**Constraints:**
- Total days: 16
- Cities & required stays:
  - Porto: 5 days
  - Prague: 4 days
  - Reykjavik: 4 days (wedding between day 4–7)
  - Santorini: 2 days
  - Amsterdam: 2 days (conference day 14–15)
  - Munich: 4 days (friend visit between day 7–10)

**Direct flights** (undirected edges):
1. Porto – Amsterdam
2. Munich – Amsterdam
3. Reykjavik – Amsterdam
4. Munich – Porto
5. Prague – Reykjavik
6. Reykjavik – Munich
7. Amsterdam – Santorini
8. Prague – Amsterdam
9. Prague – Munich

**Key fixed events:**
- Reykjavik wedding: day 4–7 → Reykjavik must include days 4,5,6,7 (4 days total).
- Amsterdam conference: day 14–15 → Amsterdam must include days 14,15.
- Munich friend visit: day 7–10 → Munich must include at least one day in 7,8,9,10.

**Approach:**
We can treat this as a scheduling + graph path problem. Since the total days sum is 5+4+4+2+2+4 = 21, but we only have 16 days, that means some days are double-counted (travel days count for both cities).  
Let’s denote travel days as days where you are in two cities.

Let’s try to place the fixed events first, then fill the rest ensuring direct flights between consecutive cities.

---

**Step 1 – Place fixed blocks:**
- Reykjavik: days 4–7 (4 days)
- Amsterdam: days 14–15 (2 days)
- Munich friend visit: choose days 8–10 (3 days) to connect with Reykjavik ending day 7.

But Munich needs 4 days total, so maybe Munich includes day 7 as well (travel from Reykjavik to Munich on day 7).

---

**Step 2 – Connect with direct flights:**
From Reykjavik (day 7) to Munich: direct flight exists (yes, Reykjavik–Munich).
From Munich to Amsterdam: direct flight exists (yes, Munich–Amsterdam).
But Amsterdam is only days 14–15, so maybe Munich stays until day 13, travel to Amsterdam on day 14 morning? That would mean Amsterdam days 14–15, then leave.

We also need Porto (5 days), Prague (4 days), Santorini (2 days).

---

**Step 3 – Sequence cities:**
We have 16 days total. Let’s try a possible route:

1. Start in Porto (5 days): days 1–5.
2. Fly Porto → Prague (direct? No, not in list). So can’t go directly.  
   Check: Porto–Munich direct exists, Porto–Amsterdam direct exists.  
   So from Porto, can go to Munich or Amsterdam.  
   But Munich is later (day 7 onward). So maybe start in Porto days 1–5, then fly to Amsterdam (direct), then Amsterdam to Prague (direct exists), but Prague needs 4 days and must connect to Reykjavik for wedding day 4–7? That’s impossible because wedding is fixed.

This means Prague must be either before or after Reykjavik wedding, with direct flight to/from Reykjavik.

Given Prague–Reykjavik direct exists, possible:  
Prague → Reykjavik (before wedding) or Reykjavik → Prague (after wedding).

But wedding is days 4–7 in Reykjavik, so if Prague is before, it must be days 1–4, then fly to Reykjavik on day 4 (counts for both). That works: Prague days 1–4, Reykjavik days 4–7.

Then from Reykjavik day 7 to Munich (direct), Munich days 7–11 (5 days? Too many, need only 4 total). Adjust: Munich days 7–10 (4 days), then Munich to Porto (direct) for Porto days 11–15 (5 days), but Amsterdam conference days 14–15 conflict with Porto. So impossible.

So Amsterdam conference must be in Amsterdam, so days 14–15 in Amsterdam.  
Thus: Munich must end by day 13, travel to Amsterdam day 14.

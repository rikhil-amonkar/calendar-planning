**Given constraints:**
- Total days: 12
- Vilnius: 4 days
- Munich: 3 days
- Mykonos: 7 days
- Direct flights exist between:
  1. Munich ↔ Mykonos
  2. Vilnius ↔ Munich
- No direct flight Vilnius ↔ Mykonos.

**Logical deductions:**
1. We must start in one city and end in another (or same), but flights must be direct.
2. Since Vilnius and Mykonos are not connected directly, any trip involving both must go through Munich.
3. The only possible sequences with direct flights are:
   - Vilnius → Munich → Mykonos
   - Mykonos → Munich → Vilnius
   (Because Vilnius only connects to Munich, and Munich connects to both.)

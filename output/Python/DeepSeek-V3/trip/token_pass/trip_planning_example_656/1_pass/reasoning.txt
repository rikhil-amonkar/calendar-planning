This means there is overlap: some days are counted twice because travel days are spent in two cities.

**Step 2 – Travel day overlap rule**  
If you fly from A to B on day X, you spend that day partly in A and partly in B, so day X counts toward both cities' required days.  
So the total trip length = sum of days in each city − number of travel days (because each travel day reduces total by 1 compared to simply summing city days).

Let T = number of travel days.  
Sum of days needed in cities = 24.  
We have 19 days total.  
So: 24 − T = 19 ⇒ T = 5 travel days.

### Step 3 – Intersect free times for 1 hour
We look for 60-minute slots in intersection of both free times on Tuesday.

Check each:  

1. Patricia free 9:00–10:00 (0–60), Jesse free 9:00–11:00 → 9:00–10:00 fits (60 minutes) → **valid slot**.  

2. Patricia free 10:30–11:00 (90–120), Jesse free 9:00–11:00 → 10:30–11:00 is only 30 minutes → not enough.  

3. Patricia free 12:00–14:00 (180–300), Jesse free:  
   - 11:30–12:00 (150–180) — no overlap with 12:00 start  
   - 12:30–13:00 (210–240) — overlaps with Patricia’s 12:00–14:00  
   - 14:00–14:30 (300–330) — no overlap with Patricia’s 12:00–14:00 except at 14:00 exactly (0 duration)  

Let’s check intersection:  
Jesse’s free on Tuesday:  
(0,120), (150,180), (210,240), (300,330), (360,390)  

Patricia’s free: (0,60), (90,120), (180,300), (420,450)

- (0,60) ∩ (0,120) = (0,60) → 60 minutes → 9:00–10:00

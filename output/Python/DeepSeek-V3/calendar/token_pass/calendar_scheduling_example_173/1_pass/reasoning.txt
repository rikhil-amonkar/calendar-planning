### Step 2 – Harold’s extra constraint
Harold doesn’t want to meet after 13:00.  
13:00 = 240 minutes from 9:00.  
So meeting must **end** by 13:00 (240).  
Since meeting is 30 minutes, it must **start** by 13:00 – 30 = 12:30 (210).  

So candidate start times: 9:00 (0) to 12:30 (210) inclusive, but must finish by 13:00.

**Step 1 – Understanding the problem**  
We start at Nob Hill at 9:00 AM.  
We have two friends with availability:  
- Kenneth: Mission District, 12:00–15:45, need at least 45 minutes together.  
- Thomas: Pacific Heights, 15:30–19:15, need at least 75 minutes together.  

Travel times are given in minutes.

**Step 2 – Time representation**  
We’ll work in minutes since 9:00 AM for easier calculations.  
- 9:00 AM = 0 minutes.  
- 12:00 PM = 180 minutes.  
- 3:30 PM = 390 minutes (since 9:00 to 15:30 is 6.5 hours = 390 min).  
- 3:45 PM = 405 minutes.  
- 7:15 PM = 615 minutes.

**Step 3 – Possible visit orders**  
We can meet Kenneth first, then Thomas, or Thomas first, then Kenneth.  
But Thomas’s window starts at 15:30, which is after Kenneth’s window ends (15:45).  
So if we meet Kenneth first, we must finish by 15:45 at latest, then travel to Pacific Heights to meet Thomas.  
If we meet Thomas first, we’d have to start at 9:00, but Thomas isn’t available until 15:30, so that’s impossible (we’d wait 6.5 hours).  
Thus, only feasible order is Kenneth → Thomas.

**Step 4 – Schedule constraints for Kenneth → Thomas**  
We start at Nob Hill at 0 min (9:00).  
Travel to Mission District: 13 minutes.  
Earliest we can meet Kenneth: 13 minutes after 9:00 = 9:13, but Kenneth is available only from 180 minutes (12:00).  
So we’ll wait until 12:00 to start meeting Kenneth.

Then we must travel to Pacific Heights for Thomas.

So we’d arrive at Pacific Heights at 12:45 + 16 = 13:01 (61 minutes after 12:45).  
But Thomas is available only from 15:30 (390 minutes). That’s a long wait (about 2.5 hours).  

Maybe we can meet Kenneth later to reduce waiting time for Thomas.

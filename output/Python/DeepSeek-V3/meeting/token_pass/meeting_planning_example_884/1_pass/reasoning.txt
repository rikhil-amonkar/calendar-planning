Let’s break this down step-by-step before writing the code.

---

## **Step 1 – Understanding the problem**

We have:

- **Start location**: Richmond District at 9:00 AM.
- **Friends** with:
  1. Location
  2. Availability window
  3. Minimum meeting duration
- **Travel times** between all locations (including Richmond as starting point).
- **Goal**: Maximize number of friends met, respecting:
  - Travel time between meetings
  - Friend’s availability window
  - Minimum meeting duration
  - Time of day constraints (can’t meet after their window ends).

---

## **Step 2 – Observations on constraints**

1. **Time windows** are strict — can only meet inside them.
2. **Travel times** are symmetric? Not exactly — checking a few:  
   Richmond to Chinatown = 20, Chinatown to Richmond = 20 (same here, but not guaranteed for all).  
   Actually, looking at data: some are different (Sunset to Richmond = 12, Richmond to Sunset = 11).  
   So travel times are **not symmetric** — must use given matrix carefully.

3. **Order matters** because travel times depend on origin/destination.

4. **Some friends have very tight windows**:
   - Deborah: 7:00 PM – 7:45 PM (45 min total) → must meet exactly 7:00–7:45 if we meet her.
   - Mark: 3:15 PM – 5:00 PM (105 min total) → needs at least 45 min.
   - Laura: 9:15 PM – 10:15 PM (60 min total) → needs at least 15 min.
   - Karen: 7:30 PM – 10:00 PM (150 min total) → needs 120 min.

5. **Long meetings** (Robert 120 min, Matthew 90 min, Karen 120 min) will block large parts of the day.

---

## **Step 3 – Strategy**

We can’t brute force all permutations of 9 friends (9! huge), but we can prune:

- Start at Richmond 9:00 AM.
- Try to schedule meetings in **time order**, respecting travel.
- Since we want to maximize *number* of friends met, maybe we should try to fit shorter meetings first?  
  But long meetings (Robert, Matthew, Karen) might be required to meet their minimums, so they need big windows.

Better approach:  
Treat as a **search problem** — try to schedule friends in sequence, at each step pick a friend not yet met, see if we can fit them after previous meeting + travel, within their window, and still have time for others later.

We can use **backtracking** with pruning.

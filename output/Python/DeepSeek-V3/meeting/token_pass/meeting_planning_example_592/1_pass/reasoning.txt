## **Step 1 — Understanding the problem**

We have:

- **Locations**: North Beach, Pacific Heights, Chinatown, Union Square, Mission District, Golden Gate Park, Nob Hill.
- **Travel times** between each pair (asymmetric).
- **Start point**: North Beach at 9:00 AM.
- **Friends** with availability windows and minimum meeting durations:

1. **James** — Pacific Heights — 20:00–22:00 — min 120 min.
2. **Robert** — Chinatown — 12:15–16:45 — min 90 min.
3. **Jeffrey** — Union Square — 9:30–15:30 — min 120 min.
4. **Carol** — Mission District — 18:15–21:15 — min 15 min.
5. **Mark** — Golden Gate Park — 11:30–17:45 — min 15 min.
6. **Sandra** — Nob Hill — 8:00–15:30 — min 15 min.

We want to meet as many friends as possible, respecting:
- Travel time between locations.
- Friend availability windows.
- Minimum meeting durations.

---

## **Step 2 — Observations**

- **James** requires 2 hours, only available 20:00–22:00, so must meet him 20:00–22:00.
- **Carol** available late evening, could meet after James? But travel from Pacific Heights to Mission District is 16 min, so if we end with James at 22:00, Carol’s window ends 21:15 — impossible. So Carol must be met *before* James.
- **Robert** needs 90 min in his window 12:15–16:45.
- **Jeffrey** needs 120 min in 9:30–15:30.
- **Mark** needs 15 min in 11:30–17:45.
- **Sandra** needs 15 min in 8:00–15:30.

We can’t meet everyone because:
- James’s meeting is fixed 20:00–22:00.
- To meet Carol before James, we must be in Mission District between 18:15–21:15 for at least 15 min, and still reach Pacific Heights by 20:00 (travel 16 min), so latest leave Mission District is 19:44.
- So Carol’s meeting must end by 19:44, start at least 15 min before that, within her window.

---

## **Step 3 — Strategy**

We’ll brute-force search over possible orders of meetings (permutations) within time constraints.

We start at North Beach at 9:00.

We’ll try to schedule all 6 friends, see if possible; if not, try subsets.

We must include travel times.

---

## **Step 4 — Approach in code**

1. Represent time in minutes from midnight for easier arithmetic.
2. Define travel time lookup table.
3. Define each friend’s location, window (start, end), min duration.
4. Start from North Beach at 9:00 (540 minutes from midnight).
5. Try permutations of friends to meet (up to all 6), respecting:
   - Arrival time at friend’s location ≥ friend window start.
   - Start meeting at max(arrival, friend window start).
   - Meeting end = start + min duration (if possible within friend’s window end).
   - If min duration doesn’t fit, skip this friend in this permutation.
   - After meeting, travel to next friend.
6. Keep track of best schedule (most friends met, then maybe total meeting time).
7. Output itinerary in required JSON format.

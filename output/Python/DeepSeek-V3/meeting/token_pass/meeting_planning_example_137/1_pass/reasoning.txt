Let’s break this down step by step before writing the code.

**Step 1 – Understanding the problem**  
We start at Financial District at 9:00.  
We have two friends:  
- Kenneth: Chinatown, 12:00–15:00, needs 90 minutes together.  
- Barbara: Golden Gate Park, 8:15–19:00, needs 45 minutes together.  

Travel times are given.  
We want to meet as many friends as possible (here, both if possible) while respecting time windows and travel.

**Step 2 – Feasibility check**  
We can meet both if we can fit travel between meetings and still meet minimum durations.

Possible order:  
1. Start at Financial District at 9:00.  
2. Go to Barbara first (Golden Gate Park) or Kenneth first (Chinatown).  

Kenneth’s window is fixed 12–15, so meeting him must be inside that, and for 90 minutes means either 12:00–13:30 or 13:30–15:00.  

Barbara’s window is large, so she’s flexible.

**Step 3 – Try meeting Barbara first**  
Leave Financial District at 9:00 → travel 23 min → arrive Golden Gate Park at 9:23.  
Meet Barbara from 9:23 to 10:08 (45 min).  
Leave at 10:08 → travel to Chinatown: 23 min → arrive 10:31.  
But Kenneth is only available from 12:00, so we’d wait 1h29m. That’s fine, but we could adjust to meet Kenneth exactly at 12:00 for 90 min, then go back to Barbara? But Barbara’s window ends at 19:00, so after Kenneth we could go back to Barbara again if time allows.

Actually, meeting both doesn’t require meeting Barbara twice. We can meet Barbara once, Kenneth once.

Better: Meet Kenneth at 12:00–13:30, then go to Barbara after.

**Step 4 – Meet Kenneth first**  
We start at Financial District at 9:00.

Meet Barbara 9:23–10:08 (45 min).  
Leave 10:08 → travel to Chinatown: 23 min → arrive 10:31.

That works: we meet both, no time conflict.

**Step 5 – Check travel times consistency**  
Golden Gate Park to Chinatown: 23 min (given). Yes, we used that.

**Step 6 – Verify no constraint violation**  
Barbara’s window: 8:15–19:00, our meet 9:23–10:08 is inside.  
Kenneth’s window: 12:00–15:00, our meet 12:00–13:30 is inside and ≥90 min.

We only need 45 min, we did exactly that. Could extend if we want, but then might risk Kenneth if traffic? But travel times fixed.  
If we extend Barbara to 11:00, then travel 23 min → arrive Chinatown 11:23, still before 12:00, so okay. But then wait longer. Let’s keep it tight to be safe.

1. Travel: Financial District → Golden Gate Park (9:00–9:23)

We’re done at 13:30, rest of day free.

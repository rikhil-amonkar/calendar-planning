We are given work hours from 9:00 to 17:00 (which is 5 PM) on Monday and Tuesday.
 We are to schedule a 1-hour meeting for Patricia and Jesse.

 Steps:
 1. Represent the available times for each person on each day, considering the work hours (9:00 to 17:00).
 2. For each day, we will generate the free intervals for each person by subtracting the busy intervals from the work hours.
 3. Then, we find the intersection of free intervals for both participants on the same day.
 4. We look for a contiguous free interval of at least 1 hour (60 minutes) in the intersection.

 Note: The time is in 24-hour format.

 We'll break the day into minutes for easier calculation, but we can also use time objects.

We are given:
 - Participants: Evelyn and Randy
 - Meeting duration: 30 minutes (half an hour)
 - Work hours: 9:00 to 17:00 on Monday.
 - Evelyn has no meetings the whole day, but does not want to meet after 13:00.
 - Randy's blocked times: 9:00-10:30, 11:00-15:30, 16:00-17:00.

 We are to find a time that works for everyone.

 Steps:
 1. We note that the meeting must be on Monday and within 9:00 to 17:00.
 2. Evelyn's constraint: available all day except after 13:00 (so only until 13:00).
 3. Randy's blocked times: 
    - 9:00-10:30 -> blocked
    - 11:00-15:30 -> blocked
    - 16:00-17:00 -> blocked

 So Randy's free times on Monday are:
    - From 10:30 to 11:00 (30 minutes)
    - From 15:30 to 16:00 (30 minutes)

 But note: Evelyn doesn't want to meet after 13:00, so the 15:30-16:00 slot is not acceptable for Evelyn.

 Therefore, the only available slot for both is 10:30 to 11:00.

 However, note that the meeting is half an hour, and the slot is exactly 30 minutes.

 Proposed meeting time: 10:30 to 11:00 on Monday.

 Let's code accordingly.

 We'll represent time in minutes from 9:00 (which is 0 minutes) to 17:00 (which is 8*60 = 480 minutes).

 Steps in code:
 1. Define the work hours: start=9:00 (0 min), end=17:00 (480 min)
 2. Represent Randy's blocked times in minutes:
    - 9:00-10:30 -> 0 to 90 minutes
    - 11:00-15:30 -> 120 to 390 minutes
    - 16:00-17:00 -> 420 to 480 minutes
 3. Randy's free intervals: [90,120] and [390,420]
 4. Evelyn's constraint: she doesn't want to meet after 13:00, which is 13:00 = 4 hours after 9:00 -> 4*60=240 minutes.
    So Evelyn is available only from 0 to 240 minutes (9:00 to 13:00).

 5. Intersection of Randy's free intervals and Evelyn's available time:
    - [90,120] is within [0,240] -> valid
    - [390,420] is outside [0,240] -> invalid

 6. Check if the interval [90,120] is at least 30 minutes: 120-90=30 -> exactly 30 minutes.

 7. So we can schedule from 90 to 120 minutes, which is 10:30 to 11:00.

 8. Convert 90 minutes to time: 9:00 + 90 minutes = 10:30.
    120 minutes = 11:00.

 9. Output: Monday 10:30:11:00

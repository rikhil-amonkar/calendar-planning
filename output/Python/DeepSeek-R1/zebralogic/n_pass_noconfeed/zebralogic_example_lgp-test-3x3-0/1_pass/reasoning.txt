 Constraints:

 1. The person who is a teacher is directly left of the person with an associate's degree.
    This means: if the teacher is in house i, then the associate's degree must be in house i+1.
    So, we need to find the house index where occupation is 'teacher', and then check that the education of the next house is 'associate'.

 2. The person with an associate's degree and Eric are next to each other.
    This means: the house with associate's degree and the house with Eric are adjacent.

 3. Peter is the person with a high school diploma.
    So, for the house where name is 'Peter', education must be 'high school'.

 4. The person who is a doctor is the person with a bachelor's degree.
    So, for the house where occupation is 'doctor', education must be 'bachelor'.

 We also have that all assignments are unique (by construction of permutations).

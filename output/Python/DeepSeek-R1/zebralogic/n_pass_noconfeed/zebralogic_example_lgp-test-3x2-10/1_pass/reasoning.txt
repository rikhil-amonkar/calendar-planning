We are given 3 houses, each with a name and a height.
 Names: Eric, Arnold, Peter
 Heights: short, very short, average

 Clues:
 1. Eric is not in the first house.
 2. The person who is very short is somewhere to the left of the person who is short.
 3. The person who is very short is Eric.
 4. Arnold is not in the first house.

 From clue 3: Eric is very short.
 From clue 2: Eric (very short) is to the left of the short person.

 Also, from clue 1 and clue 4: Neither Eric nor Arnold is in house 1, so house 1 must be Peter.

 Let's assign:
 Since Eric is very short and must be to the left of the short person, Eric cannot be in the last house (house 3) because then there would be no house to the right for the short person.

 Therefore, Eric must be in house 2 (because house 1 is not allowed for Eric, and house 3 would break clue 2).

 But wait: clue 1 and 4 say Eric and Arnold are not in house 1, so house 1 is Peter.

 Then, Eric must be in house 2 or 3. But if Eric is in house 3, then the very short person (Eric) is in house 3, but clue 2 says the very short person is to the left of the short person. So if Eric is in house 3, there is no house to the right, so he cannot be to the left of anyone. Therefore, Eric must be in house 2.

 Then, the short person must be in house 3 (since Eric is in house 2 and to the left of the short person).

 Then, Arnold must be in house 3? But wait, house 3 has the short person, and we have Eric in house 2 (very short) and Peter in house 1. So house 3 must be Arnold.

 Now, what about the heights? We have:
 House 1: Peter -> height must be average (since very short and short are taken by Eric and Arnold)
 House 2: Eric -> very short
 House 3: Arnold -> short

 This satisfies all clues.

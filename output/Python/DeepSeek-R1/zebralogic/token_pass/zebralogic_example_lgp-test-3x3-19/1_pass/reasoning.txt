 Clues:

 2. Arnold is the person who loves mystery books.
 3. The person who loves science fiction books is not in the first house.
 4. The Desert smoothie lover is directly left of the person who loves mystery books.
 5. Peter is in the first house.

 Steps:
 - Since Peter is in house 1 (clue 5), we can assign Peter to house 1.
 - From clue 2, Arnold loves mystery books. Let's denote the house of Arnold as the mystery book lover.
 - From clue 4, the Desert smoothie lover is directly left of the mystery book lover (Arnold). So if Arnold is in house N, then desert smoothie is in house N-1.
 - From clue 1, Cherry smoothie is left of Arnold (mystery books).
 - From clue 3, science fiction books are not in house 1.

   Since desert smoothie is directly left of Arnold (mystery books), Arnold cannot be in house 1 (because no house left of 1). So Arnold is in house 2 or 3.

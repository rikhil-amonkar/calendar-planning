 Constraints (clues):
 1. The person who is a doctor and Eric are next to each other.
    This means: |position(doctor) - position(Eric)| == 1

 2. The person who loves cooking is directly left of the person who is a teacher.
    So: position(cooking) = position(teacher) - 1

 3. The person who is a doctor is somewhere to the right of the person who enjoys gardening.
    So: position(doctor) > position(gardening)

    So: hobby(photography) and occupation(teacher) are the same person.

 5. The person who is an engineer is Peter.
    So: occupation(engineer) and name(Peter) are the same person.
